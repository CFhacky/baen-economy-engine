## THE STRUCTURE (why every scene points the same way)

Master Goldweaver, Senior Appraiser of the Jewellers' Guild, certified the flawless ruby behind ESAMT at **1,010,000 gp**. Every leverage operation Shi'van runs traces back to his seal. His page is explicit: if she routes appraisals to another authority, he won't confront her — he'll quietly stop appraising her work, and that would be catastrophic.

So Carat House sells **valuations, never certifications.** Every Chettiar Letter carries the line *"a valuation, not a certification."* Shi'van's own pieces always go to the House of Gems. Anything that must stand as collateral or in court is referred to the Hall, and Carat House takes no cut. Nandini joins the guild. Done this way, Carat House feeds Goldweaver's chamber instead of competing with it. Session 8B doctrine already requires it: *all stones through the guild registry, customer and family editions, no exceptions.*

## THE RATE CARD (ruled — Nandini presents it in Scene 1)

**Appraisal**
- Counter look: **2 gp** — waived if a loan or purchase follows
- Chettiar Letter (three-light grading, inclusion map, as-is and if-recut values): **10 gp** under 500 gp value · **0.5%** at 500 gp+ · 1–3 days. The guild charges 1% and takes 2–4 tendays for a certification.
- Rush: **+50%** (the guild charges +100%)
- Estate / collection survey: **25 gp** a visit + **1 gp** per item under 500 + **0.25%** on items 500+
- Identity map (a stone's inclusion "fingerprint", kept on file): **5 gp** per stone
- Referral for legal certification: **no cut**
- TSA subcontract: same as the public rate — 0.5% of stone value, min 15 gp; **10 gp** per inspection visit

**Pawn — the Carat ticket**
- Advance on Nandini's valuation: **70%** (graded stones, guild-registered pieces, B&D and Silverhair work) · **55%** (other Waterdhavian makers, ungraded quality stones) · **40%** (unknown maker, material value only). Enchantment counts as zero unless the Institut assesses it.
- Interest, per month: **3%** (10–99 gp) · **2%** (100–999) · **1.5%** (1,000–4,999) · **1%** (5,000+)
- Three months, one renewal, 30-day grace. Tickets are non-transferable. Over 25,000 gp needs Helena's co-sign.
- **Loans of 500 gp and up that forfeit are sold, and every coin of surplus above principal, interest and a 5% sale cost goes back to the borrower within 30 days.**
- Rate card **posted on the wall.**

**Wholesale and retail**
- House (Institut, B&D, Silverhair work): landed Baldur's Gate cost **+10%**
- Trade — guild jewellers only: **+30%** (a 9 gp BG fire opal lands at ~12 gp against 15–20 Waterdeep retail)
- Retail: 15–20 exceptional stones at **30–40%** over cost. Every sale ships with a free Chettiar Letter.

## SCENE 1 — THE RATE CARD
*Nandini Chettiar, Shi'van, Helena Brightwater. Nandini's rooms above the cartographer's shop, Castle Ward, or a table at NCF.*

**Nandini:** thirty-four, five-foot-five, brown skin with warm golden undertones, black braid past her shoulder blades, gold nose stud on the left, her grandmother's thin gold bangle on the right wrist. Draped fabric in saffron or oxblood, cut for a northern winter. Durpari accent, vowels held a fraction longer than Common. Tea, never coffee — black, with cardamom and milk, strong enough to stand a spoon in. The rosewood case of forty reference stones stays within reach.

She has written the schedule herself and lays it out one line at a time. Helena reads the lending terms the way a banker does, from the default clause backwards.

**The beat:** the surplus-return rule is **Nandini's condition, not Shi'van's gift.** *"If I lowball a pledge, the house keeps the difference. So we don't keep it. Otherwise every letter I sign becomes a question about whether I was buying cheap."* It is her grandmother's rule; Shi'van only has to recognise it.

**Helena's push:** the 1% top band sits above TSA's 6–8% a year — deliberately. Big borrowers who can live with an inspector in the house go to TSA; the pawn premium buys discretion. Helena wants that said out loud so TSA and Carat House never undercut each other.

## SCENE 3 — THE SENIOR MASTERS' COUNCIL
*House of Gems, second floor. Twelve-seat walnut oval, portraits of past Senior Masters, lamp-lit, leather and pipe smoke. Master Goldweaver sits on the Council.*

Shi'van sponsors Nandini on her Master Associate standing. Nandini sits a **Master panel examination on foreign standing** — the tiers were written for guild-trained jewellers, and she trained in Durpar and Baldur's Gate.

**Goldweaver:** late sixties, short and slight, white hair cropped close, beard clipped to a neat point. His loupe hangs on a silver chain under the waistcoat until he sits down to a stone. Grey eyes, perfectly still. When he has reached a conclusion he taps the table once, softly, with his right middle finger.

**His question — the scene turns on it:** *"Whose certification will your letters defer to?"* There is one right answer, and Carat House's whole design is built around it: **the Hall's.** Answered straight, he taps the table. If anyone hedges, the Council notices, and so does he.

Handle any mention of his daughter — a journeywoman gem-cutter advancing through the guild on her own — with professional respect. He does not ask for favours for her. He notices how people treat her.
