## §3 — What the last fifteen bodies added

The tail of the queue was small entities, and three of them were the
Unconfirmed rows the handoff forbids booking. Reading them confirmed the rule
rather than overturning it.

**Gold Lending House** carries its own stale-seed warning: the source design
doc uses **retired canon** — "Treasury metals" is dead (see Mennarn's Folly);
NCF's actual capital backing is the **Shimmerdeep Cradle** silver vein. Dorn
Ironlock's dwarven connection resolves to **House Ironledger**, one of the NCF
Five Pillars. Its 12 "employees" are a design roster sitting in a live headcount
column. **Lobster King** (36,500/yr) and **Waterdeep Information Brokerage**
(18,250/yr) exist only in a Campaign Manager `.tsx` artifact — exactly the class
of number the handoff forbids. All three stay at zero.

**Maritime Operations (Planned)** says of itself: *"This entity exists to make
the gap visible, not to claim the gap is filled."* The empire owns **zero
watercraft** while holding a port city, a Dock Ward warehouse, a canal inland
port and a planned mega-facility.

The **Northern Sea Gate cluster** (canal, harbour, shipyard, Seven Spires,
Thundertree) is all Year 3–8, i.e. 1497–1503 DR, and correctly carries no money.
Two things in it matter now: every page states construction neglect costs
schedule and overrun rather than revenue, and both the canal and Thundertree
pages draw the same **deliberate firebreak** around the Warborn Engineering
Campus across the river — no permanent bridge, no shared utility system, no
automatic timber right. That is consistent with Warborn being 100% Arik and
imperial household.

**Seven Spires** is logged Sector = Hospitality and states its true role is
strategic/domestic; it will never earn hospitality revenue. It also holds one of
the canal's ten locks, so it is simultaneously a retreat and infrastructure.

**The education pipeline** is four tiers — Orchard Chapel → Schola Ducum →
Collegium → Devil Academy/Institut — and the empire **does not own tier one**.
Orchard Chapel is explicitly patronage, not ownership: "the chapel retains its
own identity and authority." The Schola page then wires the whole chain into the
GM Consequence Ledger: *"When entities suffer neglect or leadership vacancies,
Schola output determines whether replacement candidates exist."* Thirty students
a year is the replacement-rate cap on every vacancy in a 90-entity registry, and
it is modelled in no canonical table.
