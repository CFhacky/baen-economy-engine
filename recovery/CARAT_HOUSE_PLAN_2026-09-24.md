## 1. Rate card (to be RULED on approval)

### Appraisal — "a valuation, not a certification" printed on every letter
| Service | Fee | Turnaround | vs House of Gems |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counter look (verbal, one piece) | 2 gp — **waived** if a loan or purchase follows | while you wait | none |
| **Chettiar Letter** (3-light grading, inclusion map, as-is and if-recut values) | 10 gp under 500 gp value · **0.5%** at 500 gp+ | 1–3 days | half the guild's 1%, ten times faster, not legally dispositive |
| Rush letter | +50% | same day | guild rush is +100% |
| Estate / collection survey (on-site) | 25 gp visit + 1 gp per item under 500 + 0.25% on items 500+ | by appointment | no guild equivalent |
| Identity map filing (inclusion-map "fingerprint", kept on file) | 5 gp per stone | with letter | new product — proves ownership of recovered stones |
| Referral for legal certification | **no cut** — client pays the guild directly | — | the goodwill channel into Goldweaver's chamber |
| TSA subcontract (inter-company) | same as public: 0.5% of stone value, min 15 gp · inspection visit 10 gp | per TSA schedule | arm's-length pricing is the self-dealing defence |

### Pawn — "the Carat ticket"
**Advance (LTV) on Nandini's valuation; enchantment counts as zero unless an Institut assessment is attached at the client's cost:**
- **Tier A — 70%:** Nandini-graded loose stones, guild-registered pieces, known makers (B&D, Silverhair)
- **Tier B — 55%:** other Waterdhavian makers, precious-metal work, ungraded quality stones
- **Tier C — 40%:** unknown maker or thin provenance, material value only

**Interest — simple, per month or part month, one month minimum:**
| Loan size | Monthly | Annualised |
|---|---|---|
| 10–99 gp | 3% | 36% |
| 100–999 gp | 2% | 24% |
| 1,000–4,999 gp | 1.5% | 18% |
| 5,000 gp+ | 1% | 12% |

The top band sits deliberately above Guardian Pledge's 6–8%: a large borrower who tolerates inspections goes to TSA; the pawn premium buys **discretion** (no inspector in the house, no lien, same-day cash). Below TSA's 3,000 gp minimum, Carat House is the only house product.

**Terms:** 3 months · one 3-month renewal on payment of accrued interest (6 months max) · 30-day grace (matches TSA) · tickets **non-transferable** (stops fences trading tickets) · single loans over 25,000 gp need Helena's co-sign.

**Forfeiture — the brand rule:** under 500 gp the item becomes house stock. **500 gp and up, the item is sold and any surplus above principal + interest + 5% sale cost is returned to the borrower within 30 days.** The house cannot profit from lowballing — the only credible proof of "won't undervalue", and the answer to any "drow pawnbroker" attack.

**Intake discipline:** name and residence on every ticket · every stone 100 gp+ inclusion-mapped at intake · any piece checked against the House of Gems registry · Nandini's Detect Lies-13 on every intake story · refuse scratched hallmarks, lots offered under 30% of value, anything on a Watch notice · rate card **posted on the wall**.

### Wholesale
- **House** (Institut, B&D, Silverhair work): landed BG cost + 10% — inter-company, nets out at ESAMT.
- **Trade** (guild-registered Waterdeep jewellers only, e.g. Farid al-Rashid): landed cost + 30%. At +30% a fire opal lands ~12 gp against 15–20 retail — cheaper for them, and they become customers instead of competitors.
- **Supply:** take over Omara's standing order; add Tobias Hunt's fire-opal claim and Ellis's Gondian diamonds on the quarterly terms already discussed.

### Retail
15–20 exceptional stones at Waterdeep retail, 30–40% over landed cost. Every sale ships with a free Chettiar Letter; stones 500 gp+ get a guild-certification referral.

## 3. Risks (new exposures)
1. **Fencing** — a pawnshop is where stolen noble jewellery goes. Intake discipline above; 30-day grace doubles as the Watch window.
2. **"Drow pawnbroker"** — hardliner material. Posted rates, the surplus-return rule, and guild membership are the defence.
3. **Guild gem dealers** — trade wholesale to guild members only, so they are supplied, not undercut.
4. **Vault concentration** — pawn goods, TSA collateral and inventory in one Castle Ward vault: a heist target. NCF team, scrying wards, the nightly count.
5. **Goldweaver** (already booked) — own pieces to the Hall, letters defer to certification, Nandini sponsored into the guild.
