## Component BOM and technical limits

Every machine requires one chassis/drive kit, core installation, Erata integration,
service kit and acceptance record. Stonebearer requires a dump bed/hydraulic
package; Groundshaper a grading/shaping package; Ironmaw an excavation/cutting
package. The latter two common architectures were proposed and ratified, not
recovered from an original machine sheet.

Monthly component demand at regular capacity is four common kits and integrations,
two Stonebearer attachments, one Groundshaper attachment and one Ironmaw attachment.
Raw material masses, alloys, losses, consumable rates and Groundshaper/Ironmaw
power and work rates remain unknown. Component counts do not establish a closed
raw-material balance or mineral consumption.

## Acceptance and service

Each machine needs serial/chassis identity, core/integration record, responsible
workshop, cost record and signed acceptance. Test drive, steering, braking,
control isolation, attachment function and road-mode conversion. Stonebearer
loaded trials must respect both 30,000 lb and 15 cubic yards. Record defects and
retest before admission. Groundshaper/Ironmaw rated specifications remain to be
established before quantitative work-output claims.

Retain the sourced twelve Brickworks drivers and two mechanics. Establish eight
service positions for the other sixteen machines using the sourced 1:2 ratio
as an adopted planning comparator. Establish fourteen primary operators for the
working field pool, covering one shift. Further shifts need more staff.

Spares cover running gear, seals/hoses, wear/cutting parts, controls and specialist
assemblies. Item quantities and opening stock are unresolved.
